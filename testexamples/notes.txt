//notes for trustgaurd extensions

//enter_untrusted -> ENTER_UNTRUSTED

//files touched and how
1) RISCVInstrInfo.td has an include for custom file RISCVInstrInfoTG.td
2) RISCVInstrInfoTG.td has custom instruction definition and pattern matching support
the above are located in /home/enemery/llvm-project/llvm/lib/Target/RISCV

3)IntrinsicsRISCV.td has intrinsic definition for llvm intrinsic
above located in /home/enemery/llvm-project/llvm/include/llvm/IR

4)RISCV.cpp has switch case for matching clang builtin to llvm intrinsic
/home/enemery/llvm-project/clang/libCodeGen/TargetBuiltins/

5)BuiltinsRISCV.td" has the trustgaurd builtin for clang for enter_untrusted defined
above is in /llvm-project/clang/include/clang/Basic/

//how to call it
__builtin_riscv_enter_untrusted();

//how to build it (current version)
1) to assembly: ~/llvm-project/build/bin/clang -target riscv64 -march=rv64gc -S t.c -o test_enter_untrusted.s
2) to IR: ~/llvm-project/build/bin/clang -target riscv64 -march=rv64gc -emit-llvm -S t.c -o test_enter_untrusted.ll
